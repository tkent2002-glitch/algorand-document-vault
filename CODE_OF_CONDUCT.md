# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in Algorand Document Vault.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions from contributors with different experience levels.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Protect private or security-sensitive information.
- Respect project scope and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Publishing another person's private information
- Deliberately disruptive or deceptive participation
- Public disclosure of unpatched vulnerabilities
- Abusive, sexualized, or intimidating language or imagery

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and other project-related spaces.
